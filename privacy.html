Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Crook Lives ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit crooklives.com (the "Site").

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and any files you attach when you use our contact form
  • Your email address if you sign up for our newsletter

We also automatically collect some technical information when you visit the Site, such as your browser type, general location, and how you interact with our pages, through standard analytics and cookies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your messages and inquiries, send updates about specials, drops, and events if you've signed up to receive them, and understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it.

Cookies

We use cookies to analyze website traffic and optimize your experience on the Site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect how the Site functions.

Third-Party Links and Services

The Site links out to third parties, including our Printify storefront for purchases, and external sites featured on our Friends of Crook Lives and Other Graffiti Stuff pages. We don't control these third parties and aren't responsible for their content or privacy practices — please review their own policies before sharing information with them.

Our contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to help prevent spam. Use of reCAPTCHA is subject to Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Data Retention & Security

We keep the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you or maintain our records, and we take reasonable steps to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send. To ask us about the information we hold about you, or to request it be corrected or deleted, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
